Request leave

Partial-day requests can only cover a single date. Set From and To to the same day to use this option.

File Certificate of Attendance

File a COA for a past day to set or correct your attendance times — whether you forgot to clock in/out or simply clocked in or out at the wrong time. Every request needs a reason and is subject to admin approval.

Lunch time is excluded from worked hours. For an overnight shift, a lunch that crosses midnight is handled automatically.

Terminate employee

Terminating immediately revokes their ability to log in. Their historical timesheets, leaves, and invoices remain on record — you can still find them later under the Terminated filter on this page.

This takes effect immediately. They'll be signed out of any active session and blocked from signing back in.
Add holiday

On a holiday, employees with no time entry are tagged Holiday instead of Absent. Paid holidays appear as Holiday hours on the invoice. If someone did clock in that day (or filed a COA), their actual hours are used as normal.

Dispute invoice

Tell your admin what looks wrong — for example the hours, the rate, the period, or the bank details. They'll review and, if needed, revise and re-send the invoice.
